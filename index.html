( research · undergraduate thesis )

Does AC ripple age a lead-acid battery faster?

Three 6 V, 4.5 Ah batteries, 35 charge cycles, the same 0.83 A RMS charging current at 7.5–8.5 V. Only the shape of the current changed.

(battery 1)

Constant DC

The control: a clean direct-current charge

(battery 2) · fastest degradation

DC + 100 Hz AC

Largest rise in internal resistance, steepest loss of discharge capacity, and the least consistent voltage behaviour

(battery 3)

DC + 1 kHz AC

The same ripple at a higher frequency

Published at IEEE GlobConET 2023 · supervised by Fahim Faisal and Mirza Muntasir Nishat, IUT · funded by an IUT research grant
